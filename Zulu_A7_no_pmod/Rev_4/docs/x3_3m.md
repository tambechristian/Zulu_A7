# Zulu_a7_no_pmod Rev 4: X3 = 3M 2908-05WB-MG microSD socket (2026-11-24)

Rev 4 is Rev 3 (committed f10e186) with the microSD socket X3 changed from the Hirose DM3D-SF (push-pull, 11.6 x 12.1 mm)
to the **3M 2908-05WB-MG** (push-push, 14.0 x 14.2 x 1.85 mm; datasheet `Datasheet/3M_2900_sd_card_socket_ts2197.pdf`,
TS-2197-02).

## Footprint

`3M-2908-05WB-MG` in `zulu_a7.PcbLib`, drawn by `tools/NoPmodX3_3M.pas` from the vector geometry of the
"recommended PCB pattern layout" (sheet 3; scale 6.4364 pt/mm, checked against every dimension):

- **Contacts 1-8:** 0.80 x 1.50 at 1.10 pitch. #2 sits 0.40 toward the rear; #4 and #6 sit 0.20 toward the mouth.
- **G1/G2 lock pins:** 1.40 x 1.90 at the mouth corners.
- **G3/G4 shell tabs:** 1.80 x 1.40 at the rear.
- **All four G pads:** GND.

An independent review re-derived the pattern from the PDF and confirmed it is a top view, not mirrored, with pin 1 at
the right end. Pad deviation is 0.00 mm. The microSD pinout is 1 DAT2, 2 DAT3, 3 CMD, 4 VDD, 5 CLK, 6 VSS, 7 DAT0,
8 DAT1.

## Schematic (`zulu_a7_3.SchDoc`)

- X3 parameters updated: 3M, MPN, Mouser number, SPEC and footprint model.
- The unconnected Hirose card-detect pins A/B and their No-ERC marks were removed, along with the Hirose order code.
- The sheet note was rewritten.

There is no card-detect switch. DAT3 has R34's 4.7 k pull-up, so card presence is found in firmware (CMD0 time-out),
as in Rev 3, where A/B were never wired. Scripts: `tools/x3_3m_sheet3.py`, `tools/x3_3m_sheet3_notes.py`.

## Routing

`tools/route/x3_3m/`, applied by `FabBCopper_X3_3M`, `_X3Btn`, `_X3Pass2` and `_X3Pass3`:

- **Pass 1:** 38 connections re-routed. VBATT and LD4_A were routed first, then the rest.
- **BTN +0.25 mm east:** done to give its body 0.80 mm from X3's.
- **Pass 2, after the review:**
  - 3M's copper-prohibition strip is at board x 5.10..18.00, y 19.35..19.95, under the shell's GND flange; my first
    pass had it 0.49 mm off. A 0.2 mm USB5V0 Top track and a through via ran inside it and were moved out.
  - The GND through via 0.075 mm from G1 was moved.
- **Pass 3:** the GND through via tangent to G1's east edge was moved. Every through via is now at least 0.63 mm from
  G1, so solder cannot wick off the load-bearing lock joint.

## Open points (not changed)

- **Release files are stale:** Rev_4 still holds Rev 3's fab and assembly packages, Gerbers and schematic PDF. Rebuild
  them before ordering.
- **JTAG holes:** a card passes over JP3/JP4 (DNS bare holes for pogo/flying-lead JTAG) and, when locked, covers
  them. Pogo access works without a card; a soldered header or leads would block the card.
- **VDD decoupling:** the socket VDD pad has no local 0.1 uF / 10 uF. The nearest VCC3V3 caps are 7 mm away;
  Rev 3's C80 happened to sit 1.4 mm away. A push-push card is hot-plugged, so a local cap is standard practice.
- **Copper under the contact springs:** two VU through vias and a VU stub sit under the open contact-spring slots of
  pins 1-2. They are tented and plugged (fab note), but moving them out is the conservative choice.
- **Library outline:** the library footprint's Mechanical 3 outline is the plain 14.0 x 14.2 box, without the
  housing tab. The board's 3D body and `body_mech.json` do include it.
- **Card travel datum:** the card positions in the placement figure use the 14.20 body edge. The datasheet measures
  them from the lock housing, 1.0 mm further out: a locked card ends at board x 0.90, an ejected one 2.9 mm past the
  edge.
