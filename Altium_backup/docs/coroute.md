# SDRAM bus + XADC inputs — routed and placed, 2026-09-15

**Status: PLACED, SAVED, DRC-CLEAN ON EVERY GEOMETRIC RULE.** The 39 SDRAM nets and the four
XADC auxiliary inputs AIN15_N/P and AIN16_N/P were routed together and placed by
`PlaceCoRoute` (`tools/ZuluSetup.pas`, generated by `tools/route_emit.py` from
`tools/coroute_plan.json`). `RemoveCoRoute` takes out the 583 added primitives and restores the
two it removed.

## Why the bus was not routed alone

A first routing run (three routers, a judge) produced three plans that each joined all 39 SDRAM
nets cleanly; the chosen one (`tools/sdram_route_plan.py`, 68 vias, 758 mm) is committed but was
**never placed**. A foreclosure audit written for the purpose (`tools/route_foreclosure.py`)
showed that every one of the three plans would leave **AIN16_N and AIN16_P with no reachable via
slot** — the D10/D11/D13 Top heads leave U1 through the same 1.5 mm window, between the fan-out's
VCC3V3 neck via (40.90, 11.90) and GND neck via (40.90, 13.40), and pass both AIN16 stub ends.
**AIN15_N was already sealed by the fan-out**, between F1's GND via (41.40, 13.8999) and G1's 0.20
GND stub. These are the inputs the R12–R17 / C36–C37 filter block exists for.

F1's via is redundant — F1 is chained to E1, which has its own via — so removing it with its
stub unseals AIN15_N. The bus was then re-routed *with* the four AIN nets.

## The plan

Judged from three co-routing strategies (local repair of the first plan / XADC first / pair
quality), each checked by an independent refuter with an analog lens:

- **repair** — rejected: a real 0.0893 mm clearance fault (AIN16_N to R11-1 GND, rule 0.09) and
  R12-2 (NODE_P0) sealed.
- **quality** — rejected: passed both gates as they then stood, but sealed four filter pads
  (R12-2, R10-1, R10-2, R16-2), disconnecting the AIN inputs from their source nodes, and ran
  19 mm under U1 on the Bottom layer the power feeds need.
- **xadc-first** — chosen. The XADC copper is placed first: U1 escapes on Top to vias at AIN15_N
  (40.60, 14.05), AIN16_P (40.45, 13.40), AIN16_N (41.30, 12.665); AIN15_P starts at its G3 moat
  via. Both pairs run on **Bottom over the L5 GND plane as a four-track bundle** down U1's west
  strip; AIN15 turns south-west to C36/R12/R13, AIN16 south-east to R16/R17/C37, each pair taking
  one Top hop where P and N swap sides at the filter. The SDRAM bus is then re-routed around it:
  south via row at y 4.40, D10/D11 heads bent around the AIN16_N via, D13 on its own via at
  (40.60, 12.75); every SDRAM net stays on one inner layer, SDRAM Bottom copper is only the U3
  pad stubs, the clock's vias keep ≥ 0.205 mm.

**75 vias, 508 tracks, 823.9 mm; one removal** (F1's GND via and its 0.20 Top stub).

XADC as measured by the judge on the placed copper: loop area U1-to-cap AIN15 5.47 mm², AIN16
4.36 mm² (the smallest of the three candidates); pair adjacency within 0.15 mm edge gap 48 % /
57 %; 3.4 mm under the U1 land field on Bottom; no SDRAM track within 0.5 mm on Bottom; AIN16_P
passes D13's Top head at 0.0905 mm for 1.60 mm — a consequence of the J2/H1/H2 ball order, the
same in every candidate.

## The gates, and the two blind spots this run exposed

1. `route_emit.py <plan> CoRoute --require-complete` — clearances per layer, widths, via pitch,
   removals matched exactly, every U1 power/GND ball still on a via, and **every pad of all 43
   nets joined**. It sampled segment-to-pad distance at 25 points and missed the repair plan's
   0.0893 mm fault; it is now closed-form (checked against a 20 000-point brute force) and
   catches it.
2. `route_foreclosure.py <plan>` — every unrouted U1 signal escape keeps a reachable legal via
   slot (flood fill of Top free space), **and every SMD pad of an unowned net within 1.5 mm of the
   plan keeps a way out** on its own layer (a legal via slot, no via-in-pad, or own-net copper).
   It audited only U1 escapes, which is how the quality plan passed; it now flags exactly that
   plan's four pads. Its window grows while a pad's free region runs into the window edge: at a
   fixed 2.4 mm it wrongly called R16-2 sealed on the chosen plan, whose pocket continues to 25
   via slots.

Because `tools/coroute_plan.py` routes with `route_emit`'s geometry, the exact distance changed
how 11 SDRAM address nets thread the U3 pocket (each within 0.02 mm, same layers); vias, the
removal and every XADC track are identical to the judged plan. The committed plan was generated
from `tools/route_inputs.json` as of commit 9d73469; since placement that file describes the
routed board, so re-running the generator now would collide with its own copper.
