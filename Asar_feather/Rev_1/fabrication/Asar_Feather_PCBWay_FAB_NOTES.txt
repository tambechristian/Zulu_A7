This data set is released for quotation and CAM/engineering review only. It
depends on PCBWay's answers to our engineering enquiry about 0.10 mm laser
via-in-pad on the 0.5 mm-pitch BGA lands, 3/3 mil, resin-plugged through vias
in SMD pads, stacking and hole spacing, which are still outstanding. Every
assumption that enquiry asks about is restated in the next section with the
counts of THIS board. The enquiry quoted counts of an older layout (before the
LTC3556 power-block change); where they differ, the enquiry's figure is shown
in brackets and the figure in these notes is the one that applies. Please
answer each numbered item, or confirm it, before fabrication is released.
