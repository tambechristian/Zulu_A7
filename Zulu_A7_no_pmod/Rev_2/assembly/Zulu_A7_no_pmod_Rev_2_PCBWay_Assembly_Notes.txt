CPL AND ORIENTATION

Rotation.
It lists the SMT parts only; X2 is hand-soldered and is not in it.
If your line needs one file per side, please split it by the Layer column.
- Units are millimetres ("mm" suffix). Origin: the board's lower-left
  corner; the outline runs from 0,0 to 60.96,25.40.
- Coordinates on both sides are as viewed from the top. Bottom-side X
  values are NOT mirrored.
- Mid X/Y is the centre of each part's pad extents, not the footprint
  origin. For connectors (X1, X3, X4) align the part to its pads in CAM.
- Rotations are the values stored in the final Altium PCB, in degrees
  (0/90/180/270), not converted for bottom-side parts. Please confirm your
  bottom-side convention against the bottom drawing before programming.
- The top drawing is viewed from the top; the bottom drawing is mirrored,
  viewed from the bottom. Red dot = pin 1/A1; red bar = LED cathode (K);
  dashed red label = DNP. The silkscreen has no polarity marks, so the
  drawings and this file are the orientation reference.
If the CPL and a drawing disagree, please send an engineering query.

SPECIAL COMPONENT INSTRUCTIONS
- Reflow order: bottom side FIRST, top side LAST. X1 is rated for one
  reflow cycle and U1 should see only one. Then hand-solder X2 and
  X1's shell stakes.
- X2 is not one 40-pin part: four single-row 2.54 mm THT strips mounted on the
  underside, 4 x Sullins PRPC010SAAN-RC, 1x10 (X2 pins 1-10, 11-20, 21-30 and
  31-40). The BOM has one line for them: designator X2, Qty 4. Housings on the
  BOTTOM side; insert the SHORT post end (about 3 mm) up through the board so
  about 1.4 mm stands on top for soldering; the LONG mating end points down.
  Solder on top by hand or selectively, after both reflows and depanel.
  Fit check: in plan view the housings overlap the pads of C87, C88, C94, C99,
  C106, R1 (worst 0.14 mm) and come within 0.1 mm of C3, C7, C8, C36, C37,
  C139, L4, L5, L6, L7, R10, R11. Seat each strip without force, check that it
  sits flush on the first article, and report any interference rather than
  forcing it.
  If you cannot quote X2, leave it unpopulated, do not buy the strips,
  and say so in the quotation.
- JP3 and JP4 are intentional DNS flying-lead/pogo positions (DNS rows in
  the BOM): bare plated holes, no paste, leave empty. They form the JTAG
  column along the left (west) board edge.
- R119-R134 (RC0201FR-0722RL, 22 ohm 0201, 11 bottom: R119, R120, R121, R122,
  R123, R124, R125, R126, R128, R133, R134; 5 top: R127, R129, R130, R131,
  R132) are SI-critical series resistors on the SDRAM data lines. Place each
  one exactly per the CPL (position, side and rotation); do not move,
  substitute or omit any of them. The 5 top ones sit in a tight column beside
  U1 (0.10-0.16 mm solder-mask webs). Pads R128-1, R128-2, R131-1, R131-2,
  R132-2, R133-1, R134-1 carry vias that the fabrication notes ask to be
  filled and capped (via-in-pad): if they are not, please tell us before
  assembly (solder wicking and tombstoning risk).
- Via-in-pad, whole board: 84 microvia sites on 83 pads of 40 parts (U8 pad 7
  carries two) (BTN, C11, C13, C80, C82, C84, C148, C150, C159, C160, C163,
  C164, C166, C167, L9, LD0, LD1, LD2, LD3, R4, R24, R34, R90, R92, R99, R103,
  R107, R108, R113, R114, R115, R116, R118, R128, R131, R132, R133, R134, U8,
  X3); the fabrication notes (VIA-IN-PAD table) ask for every one to be
  copper-filled and capped flat. If any arrive unfilled, please tell us before
  assembly.
- X1 (Molex 105017-0001 micro-USB-B, top side) opens toward the upper
  board edge. Its shell stakes go into the plated 0.60 x 1.30 mm slots
  MS1/MS2 at the mating-face end, beside the board edge. The MP1/MP4
  paste only tacks them: please hand-solder MS1/MS2 from the bottom side
  after reflow to
  full barrel fill. Please confirm from the Molex drawing whether the
  MH1/MH2 locating pegs are to be soldered.
- X3 (Hirose DM3D-SF microSD, top side): the card opening faces the left
  board edge.
- X4 (JST B2B-PH-SM4-TB, top side): pin 1 (the west contact) is battery
  positive, pin 2 is GND.
- U8 (LTC3556EUFD#PBF, Analog Devices, bottom side): QFN-28 4 x 5 mm, 0.5 mm
  pitch, exposed pad (pin 29) to GND. Pin 1 per the drawing's red dot.
- U7 (Semtech SC189ASKTRT, SOT-23-5, bottom side): 1.0 V FPGA core buck.
- Inductors L1, L2, L3, L8, L9 (bottom side) share one 2.5 x 2.0 mm body and
  are usually unmarked: L1, L2, L9 = DFE252012F-2R2M=P2 (2.2 uH), L3 =
  DFE252010P-1R5M=P2 (1.5 uH), L8 = DFE252012F-3R3M=P2 (3.3 uH). Load each
  position from its own reel and check the reels on the first article.
- LD1, LD2, LD3, LD4 (0603): cathode = pad K on the EAST side (X 23.00 mm);
  all face the same way. The drawings mark it with a red bar.
- LD0 (Everlight 19-337/R6GHBHC-A01/2T, 1.6 x 1.6 mm): cathodes are on the WEST side,
  the cathode-mark column (board footprint pads 2, 4, 6), anodes on the east
  side (pads 1, 3, 5), opposite to the 0603 LEDs. The drawing's red dot marks
  footprint pad 1, an anode. The land is symmetric, so a part turned 180
  degrees fits but stays dark.
  Its datasheet's pin numbers depend on the revision: Rev.4 (DSE-0002657)
  numbers the cathodes 1-3 and the anodes 4-6, Rev.6 numbers them like the
  footprint. Follow the cathode mark, not the pad numbers.
- Verify pin 1/A1 against the drawings on Q1, U1, U2, U3, U4, U7, U8, U10 and X4.
- Stencil from the GTP/GBP layers, whose apertures are 1:1 with copper.
  Please apply your house reductions, in particular: U2 LQFP-64 0.5 mm
  pitch; U8 exposed pad (about 85 % area, windowpane); U1 0.225 mm round
  apertures need a thin stencil (0.08 mm or thinner suggested) and a
  paste that releases them reliably.
