What changed

and what each one is worth
1

SPI flash to the front, above the FPGA

It sat at the far left of the back while the only thing it talks to is the FPGA. Its six nets are on fixed balls — D18, D19, F18, G18, K19, C11 — so no re-pin could ever have shortened them.

−234 mm of net length

2

XADC dividers beside the FPGA

Same problem: fixed balls G2, G3, H2, J2 feeding dividers 45 mm away. Moving them also puts the long run on the raw header signal instead of the high-impedance divided node.

−169 mm of net length

3

Bucks to the far left, EEPROM behind the bridge

Three switching nodes sat 0.4 mm from a 16-bit memory bus. The power section takes the whole left end under the microSD's shell; the EEPROM moves to where it belongs, behind the FT2232.

1.7 mm clearance, not 0.4

4

Bottom row renumbered 21–40

The drawing still carried the old 44-pin field. X2 is 40 pins: 21–29 right of the LiPo landing, 30–40 left of it, mirroring the top row exactly.

40 pins, contiguous

5

U1 orientation tested, and left alone

A stack review proposed rotating the FPGA so Bank 14 faced the SDRAM, on the premise that the 39-net bus is a Bank 14 bus. It is not: it sits on bank 34 (22 nets) and bank 35 (15), which already occupy the west and south edges — the SDRAM side. Bank 14 carries the X2 channels. Scoring every net with one U1 ball against its real destination, per header pin rather than per part, gives 0° 1730 mm total (591 mm of it SDRAM), 270° 1758, 90° 1925, and 180° 1945 — the proposed rotation is the worst of the four, and lengthens the timing-critical bus by 42% to shorten the header channels by 10%.

Still open. The SDRAM stays beside the FPGA rather than behind it, because that choice belongs to the stack: with through-vias the dog-bones litter the back under the ball field and the SDRAM cannot sit there, but with 1+6+1 HDI the back is clean and 38 lateral nets become 38 short drops. The four landing positions — 36.83, 34.29, 31.75, 29.21 — line up on both rows, so that 10 mm window is the one place a signal crosses from row to row without threading the pin fence. Keep it out of the pours.
