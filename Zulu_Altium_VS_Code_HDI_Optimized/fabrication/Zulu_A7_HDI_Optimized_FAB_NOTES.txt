HDI CONSTRUCTION
The drill data requires a symmetric two-step sequential buildup:
  Top -> L2       0.15 mm laser microvia
  L2 -> L3        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L3 -> L4        0.15 mm mechanically drilled buried via
  L4 -> L5        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L5 -> Bottom    0.15 mm laser microvia
  Top -> Bottom   0.20 mm plated through via

Stacked adjacent-layer microvias occur at common XY locations. Apply JLCPCB's
required copper fill and planarization process for stacked microvias.

X1 MOLEX 105017-0001 PLATED SLOTS
X1-MS1 and X1-MS2 are two plated, vertical 0.60 mm x 1.30 mm full-radius
front shell slots (mating-face end). Their centers are (29.5199, 23.9585) mm and
(36.5201, 23.9585) mm. The dedicated SlotHoles file uses a 0.60 mm tool
over a 0.70 mm G85 centerline route to produce the 1.30 mm overall length.
Do not convert these slots to round holes or treat them as non-plated.
Slot lands: 1.11 x 1.81 mm oblong on Top and Bottom (0.255 mm ring),
0.90 x 1.60 mm oblong on L2-L5. Other-net copper is at least 0.247 mm
from the slot walls on every layer.
