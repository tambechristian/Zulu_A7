FABRICATOR
PCBWay, bare-board fabrication. Order as Material "HDI (Buried/blind vias)"
with Custom stackup ticked and impedance control ticked. Online HDI pricing
covers 1+N+1 only, so this two-step build needs a manual quotation and
engineering review before release.

HDI CONSTRUCTION (sequential 1+1+N+1+1, symmetric)
  Top -> L2       0.15 mm laser microvia
  L2 -> L3        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L3 -> L4        0.15 mm mechanically drilled buried via (in the core)
  L4 -> L5        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L5 -> Bottom    0.15 mm laser microvia
  Top -> Bottom   0.20 mm plated through via
Stacked adjacent-layer microvias share XY locations: copper-fill and planarize
them ("Via filled with copper"). The vias around U1 (0.5 mm BGA) are
interstitial, NOT via-in-pad; no resin-filled via-in-pad is required.

TARGET STACKUP (as modelled in the PCB file; please return your proposed
manufacturable stack with pressed thicknesses and Dk for approval)
  L1  Top          copper 0.035
      Prepreg      0.112   (2116-class; design assumed Dk 4.29)
  L2  GND          copper 0.030
      Prepreg      0.112
  L3  Signal       copper 0.030
      Core         0.930   (Dk 4.6 assumed)
  L4  Signal       copper 0.030
      Prepreg      0.112
  L5  VCC3V3       copper 0.030
      Prepreg      0.112
  L6  Bottom       copper 0.035
  Laminate about 1.57 mm; about 1.63 mm with solder mask. Inner copper 1 oz.
The outer prepregs carry the 0.15 mm laser vias, so keep them at or below
about 0.12 mm (1:1 laser aspect ratio).

CONTROLLED IMPEDANCE
USB_D_P / USB_D_N: 90 ohm differential, +/-10 %.
Top layer, 0.150 mm trace / 0.150 mm gap, referenced to L2 GND.
Please return the calculated impedance on your stack; trace width may be
adjusted by your CAM within tolerance.

BOARD
Finished outline 69.85 mm x 25.40 mm. Layer order:
  L1  Top
  L2  GND plane polygon (positive plot)
  L3  Signal
  L4  Signal
  L5  VCC3V3 plane polygon (positive plot)
  L6  Bottom
The L2 and L5 Gerbers are positive polygon plots, not negative plane files.

VIA AND HOLE GEOMETRY
Laser microvias: 0.15 mm hole / 0.30 mm land
L3-L4 buried vias: 0.15 mm hole / 0.27 mm land
Signal through vias: 0.20 mm hole / 0.35 mm land
Component holes: 0.85 mm (X1 MH1/MH2) and 1.016 mm (headers), plated.
There are no non-plated holes.

PLATED SLOTS (X1, Molex 105017-0001 micro-USB-B rear shell legs)
Two plated slots, 0.60 mm wide x 1.30 mm long overall, long axis
perpendicular to the board edge, centred at X 29.520 / Y 23.959 mm and
X 36.520 / Y 23.959 mm. They are in zulu_a7-SlotHoles.TXT as G85 drilled
slots (0.60 mm tool, 0.70 mm between end centres). They are oval; do not
make them round. There are no slots on GKO/GM1. Lands: 1.11 x 1.81 mm oblong
on Top and Bottom (0.255 mm ring), 0.90 x 1.60 mm on the inner layers.

ITEMS FOR ENGINEERING REVIEW (the design was drawn to tighter limits than
some of your published "normal" values; please confirm or propose changes)
  - Minimum track / space 0.0762 / 0.09 mm (3.0 / 3.5 mil) on all layers.
  - Via annular rings: through 0.075 mm, laser 0.075 mm, buried 0.06 mm.
  - Different-net via hole-to-hole down to 0.24 mm.
  - 0.20 mm through vias in a board of about 1.63 mm (aspect about 8:1).
  - Laser and buried via holes to inner plane copper about 0.165 mm
    (0.075 mm ring + 0.09 mm clearance).
