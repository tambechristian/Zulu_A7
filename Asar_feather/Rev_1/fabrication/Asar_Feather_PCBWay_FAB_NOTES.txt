*** PROVISIONAL RELEASE - DO NOT START PRODUCTION ***
This data set is released for quotation and CAM/engineering review only. It
depends on PCBWay's answers to our engineering enquiry about 0.10 mm laser
via-in-pad on the 0.5 mm-pitch BGA lands, 3/3 mil, resin-plugged through vias
in SMD pads (none left now, see Q8), stacking and hole spacing. PCBWay
answered it on 2026-11-16; the points that answer left open are in our
follow-up of the same date and are still outstanding. Every assumption that
enquiry asks about is restated in the next section with the counts of THIS
board. The enquiry quoted counts of an older layout (before the LTC3556
power-block change); where they differ, the enquiry's figure is shown in
brackets and the figure in these notes is the one that applies. Please answer
each numbered item, or confirm it, before fabrication is released.

This data set replaces the 2026-11-21 Asar packages; do not mix files of
different packages. It takes every through via out of the SMD pads, so no
through via needs resin plugging (Q8): the 102 through vias that sat in or on
a pad were moved out to a dog-bone (land at least 0.076 mm from pad copper,
hole at least 0.10 mm from the mask opening) or replaced by filled laser vias
in the pad. Through vias 153 -> 109, laser microvias 1174 -> 1359, buried
L3-L4 vias 254 -> 311; six small parts moved by 0.17 mm or less (C78, C121,
C124, L1, L2, L9; all on the bottom side). Copper on all six layers, the
bottom solder mask and bottom paste (the 12 pads of the moved parts) and the
plated drill files TXT, TX3, TX6, TX7, TX9 and TX10 change; the top mask and
paste, the non-plated and slot drill files, the board outline, stack-up and
silkscreen do not.
