## Method and limits

- **Copper.** board_extract.json with d13/final_plan.json applied by the simulator's plan-JSON reader (renames = remove + re-add). Each net is split at its R into SDRAM-Dn (U3 pin .. R pad 1) and Dn (R pad 2 .. U1 ball); each half is netlisted by bus_sim's net_elements(), and both halves are connected in every case.
- **R model.** R plus 0.5 nH in series, 0.05 pF across, 0.08 pF from each pad to ground (parts.md estimate, the same as d10/sim).
- **Stacks.** Fab = the PCBWay construction (L3/L4 at 0.125 mm = 43.4 ohm). PcbDoc = the stack stored in the PcbDoc (thicker inner dielectrics, 49.1 ohm).
- **Corners.** IBIS typ and max at 3.3 / 3.465 V; the IBIS slow corner was not run.
- **Not modelled.** Coupling (including the SDRAM-D13 Top run under U2 and the x 38.5-40.2 Top bundle); plane openings, including the new L2/L5 voids (planes are ideal); 0201 package uncertainty.
