ORDER AND CAM GATE
This is a bare-board, six-layer, two-step sequential HDI release. It is a
smaller derivative of the Zulu A7 Fab B Rev 2 board: same stack, same layer
use, same via types and sizes, same design rules. What changed: the Pmod
header and its parts were removed, the board was cut to 60.96 x 25.40 mm,
the JTAG holes moved, 16 SDRAM series resistors were added, and the title is
ZULU A7 2.3 (see CHANGES FROM FAB B REV 2). The drill and via counts below
are new. Only microvias sit in SMD pads; no through via sits in any SMD pad
(see VIA-IN-PAD).
Route it to PCBWay HDI engineering/CAM review. Do not release fabrication as
a standard six-layer prototype without confirming every blind/buried drill
pair, the via-in-pad list and the ENGINEERING QUESTIONS below.

CHANGES FROM FAB B REV 2
Outline: 69.85 x 25.40 mm -> 60.96 x 25.40 mm (2.4 x 1.0 in). The Pmod
header J1 (12 plated holes) and its series resistors R26-R33 left the
design. The JTAG header holes JP3/JP4 (3 + 3, not fitted) now sit in one
column at the west short edge. Sixteen 0201 SDRAM data series resistors
R119-R134 were placed and routed (7 of their pads carry an in-pad microvia,
listed below). Five through vias that the resize and the JTAG move had left

two on the R108 pads went when R108 moved. The JTAG-column move had put R108

HDI CONSTRUCTION
The drill data requires a symmetric two-step sequential buildup:
  Top -> L2       0.15 mm laser microvia
  L2 -> L3        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L3 -> L4        0.15 mm mechanically drilled buried via
  L4 -> L5        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L5 -> Bottom    0.15 mm laser microvia
  Top -> Bottom   0.20 mm plated through via

Adjacent-layer laser microvias are stacked at common XY locations. Fill and
planarize the receiving microvias and buried vias before the next buildup
step. Confirm the complete lamination and fill/cap process in the CAM
response.

There is NO L2-L5 drill in this data. At 43 XY sites an L2-L3 microvia, an
L3-L4 buried via and an L4-L5 microvia coincide (24 of them also carry
Top-L2 and L5-Bottom microvias, i.e. full Top-to-Bottom stacks). These are
three separate drills (TX7, TX3, TX9) stacked on filled vias, exactly as
drawn. Please keep them as separate drills; do not merge them into an L2-L5
drill.
