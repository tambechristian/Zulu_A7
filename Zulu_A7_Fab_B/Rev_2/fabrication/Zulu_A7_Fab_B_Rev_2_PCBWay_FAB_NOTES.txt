ORDER AND CAM GATE
This is a bare-board, six-layer, two-step sequential HDI release. It is a
revision of the six-layer HDI Zulu A7 board PCBWay has already reviewed: same
outline, same layer use, same via types and the same agreed stack. What
changed against that board: the power block (one LTC3556 PMIC plus one SC189A
buck replace the bq24232 charger and three bucks), the LED/button row was
re-spread and moved, the USB and battery connectors and the header holes were
re-arranged (see CHANGES FROM FAB B REV 1), and the title is now ZULU A7 2.0.
The drill and via counts below are new, and this revision has many more
via-in-pad sites, all of them microvias; no through via sits in any SMD pad
(see VIA-IN-PAD).
Route it to PCBWay HDI engineering/CAM review. Do not release fabrication as
a standard six-layer prototype without confirming every blind/buried drill
pair and the via-in-pad list below.

CHANGES FROM FAB B REV 1
Rev 2 is the Fab B Rev 1 layout with the connectors re-arranged. The
micro-USB connector X1 and the LiPo connector X4 moved 2.54 mm west; X1's
plated slots and mounting holes moved with it (see X1 PLATED SLOTS). Header
X2 pads 10 and 30 moved 10.16 mm east to x 36.83 mm, so the 40 header holes
now form four 1 x 10 rows with a 12.70 mm gap around X1/X4. Four Bottom parts
(U10, R96, R84, R86) moved clear of X1's mounting holes and the copper around
the moved parts was rerouted: via objects 1,921 -> 1,961 (through vias
369 -> 354, laser microvias 1,161 -> 1,204, L3-L4 buried vias 391 -> 403).
Outline, stack, layer use, via and hole sizes, the 68 via-in-pad sites and
every design rule are unchanged from Rev 1.

HDI CONSTRUCTION
The drill data requires a symmetric two-step sequential buildup:
  Top -> L2       0.15 mm laser microvia
  L2 -> L3        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L3 -> L4        0.15 mm mechanically drilled buried via
  L4 -> L5        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L5 -> Bottom    0.15 mm laser microvia
  Top -> Bottom   0.20 mm plated through via

Adjacent-layer laser microvias are stacked at common XY locations. Fill and
planarize the receiving microvias and buried vias before the next buildup
step. Confirm the complete lamination and fill/cap process in the CAM
response.

There is NO L2-L5 drill in this data. At 47 XY sites an L2-L3 microvia, an
L3-L4 buried via and an L4-L5 microvia coincide (28 of them also carry
Top-L2 and L5-Bottom microvias, i.e. full Top-to-Bottom stacks). These are
three separate drills (TX7, TX3, TX9) stacked on filled vias, exactly as
drawn. Please keep them as separate drills; do not merge them into an L2-L5
drill.

CONTROLLED IMPEDANCE

above, with PCBWay's etch compensation.
Layer/reference: L1 Top, referenced to L2 GND
Drawn geometry: 0.15 mm trace / 0.15 mm pair gap (measured on the board:
both nets 0.15 mm wide and 15.92 mm long, 11.79 mm of parallel run at a
0.15 mm gap; the rest is the breakout at X1 and U2 and a small
length-matching jog on USB_D_P; no vias on the pair).

are not impedance controlled; do not adjust them.
