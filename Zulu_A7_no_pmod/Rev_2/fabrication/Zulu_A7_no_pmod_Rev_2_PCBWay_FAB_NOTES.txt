ORDER AND CAM GATE
This is a bare-board, six-layer, two-step sequential HDI release. It is a
smaller derivative of the Zulu A7 Fab B Rev 2 board: same stack, same layer
use, same via types and sizes, same design rules. What changed: the Pmod
header and its parts were removed, the board was cut to 60.96 x 25.40 mm,
the JTAG holes moved, 16 SDRAM series resistors were added, U2 is now an
FT2232HQ in a QFN-64 package, X4 is now a side-entry SMT header at the south
edge, and the title is ZULU A7 2.3 (see CHANGES FROM FAB B REV 2). The drill
and via counts below are new. This release SUPERSEDES the 2026-11-13 no-PMOD
Rev 2 data package (that one has an LQFP U2 and a different X4); do not mix
files of the two. Microvias sit in SMD pads, and TWO through vias also sit
in SMD pads (GND, in the U2 exposed pad and in X4 pad 2): both must be
filled and capped (see VIA-IN-PAD).
Route it to PCBWay HDI engineering/CAM review. Do not release fabrication as
a standard six-layer prototype without confirming every blind/buried drill
pair, the via-in-pad list and the ENGINEERING QUESTIONS below.

HDI CONSTRUCTION
The drill data requires a symmetric two-step sequential buildup:
  Top -> L2       0.15 mm laser microvia
  L2 -> L3        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L3 -> L4        0.15 mm mechanically drilled buried via
  L4 -> L5        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L5 -> Bottom    0.15 mm laser microvia
  Top -> Bottom   0.20 mm plated through via

Adjacent-layer laser microvias are stacked at common XY locations. Fill and
planarize the receiving microvias and buried vias before the next buildup
step. Confirm the complete lamination and fill/cap process in the CAM
response.

There is NO L2-L5 drill in this data. At 48 XY sites an L2-L3 microvia, an
L3-L4 buried via and an L4-L5 microvia coincide (26 of them also carry
Top-L2 and L5-Bottom microvias, i.e. full Top-to-Bottom stacks). These are
three separate drills (TX7, TX3, TX9) stacked on filled vias, exactly as
drawn. Please keep them as separate drills; do not merge them into an L2-L5
drill.

VIA-IN-PAD (VIPPO REQUIRED)
TWO THROUGH VIAS SIT IN SMD PADS: U2-EP at (32.350, 16.575) mm, hole across
the pad edge; X4-2 at (32.475, 9.025) mm, hole inside the pad. Both are GND
Top-Bottom vias, 0.35 mm land / 0.20 mm hole. Each sits in an SMD pad that
is soldered (the U2 exposed pad, at its corner, and X4 pad 2), so each MUST
be resin-filled and capped flat by plating (VIPPO, IPC-4761 type VII), like
the microvias below, or solder will wick into the hole. Their Bottom lands
are covered by solder mask. They are listed in the table below as "thru
via". Please confirm in the CAM response that both are filled and capped.
No other through via has its land overlapping an SMD pad on either side:
none of the other 316 Top-Bottom 0.20 mm through vias does, and every one of
their drilled holes is at least 0.075 mm outside every pad. 11 through vias
(13 pad contacts) have a land that ENDS ON the edge of a same-net SMD pad
(gap within 0.0018 mm of zero in the design data, i.e. tangent; listed below
and under VIA TENTING); they are not via-in-pad sites and are plugged with
the other through vias. Besides the two through vias above, only microvias
are in pads. This board has 143 via sites whose land overlaps an SMD pad
(143 pad contacts; no via touches two pads). At 136 sites the drilled hole
