ORDER AND CAM GATE
This is a bare-board, six-layer, two-step sequential HDI release of the Zulu
A7 Fab B Rev 2 board, 69.85 x 25.40 mm: same outline, stack, layer use and
via types and sizes as the 2026-11-11 Fab B Rev 2 data, and the same
fabrication design rules except one new solder-mask rule (U2's pads use a
0.035 mm mask expansion instead of the board's 0.05 mm, see ENGINEERING
QUESTIONS); two new component-clearance rules (R131/R132 and X2) concern
assembly only. It SUPERSEDES the 2026-11-11 Fab B Rev 2 data package; do not
mix files of the two. What changed (see CHANGES FROM THE 2026-11-11
RELEASE): U2 is now an FT2232HQ in a QFN-64 package with a grounded exposed
pad, X4 is now a side-entry SMT header at the south edge, U10 is now an
MSOP-8, 16 SDRAM series resistors were added, 11 parts were placed
differently, and the area around them was re-routed. The drill and via
counts below are new. Microvias sit in SMD pads; NO through via sits in any
SMD pad (see VIA-IN-PAD).
Route it to PCBWay HDI engineering/CAM review. Do not release fabrication as
a standard six-layer prototype without confirming every blind/buried drill
pair, the via-in-pad list and the ENGINEERING QUESTIONS below.

CHANGES FROM THE 2026-11-11 RELEASE

exposed pad EP, which is GND and carries 10 in-pad laser microvias) instead

HDI CONSTRUCTION
The drill data requires a symmetric two-step sequential buildup:
  Top -> L2       0.15 mm laser microvia
  L2 -> L3        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L3 -> L4        0.15 mm mechanically drilled buried via
  L4 -> L5        0.15 mm laser microvia
  L5 -> Bottom    0.15 mm laser microvia
  Top -> Bottom   0.20 mm plated through via

Adjacent-layer laser microvias are stacked at common XY locations. Fill and
planarize the receiving microvias and buried vias before the next buildup
step. Confirm the complete lamination and fill/cap process in the CAM
response.

There is NO L2-L5 drill in this data. At 53 XY sites an L2-L3 microvia, an
L3-L4 buried via and an L4-L5 microvia coincide (32 of them also carry
Top-L2 and L5-Bottom microvias, i.e. full Top-to-Bottom stacks). These are
three separate drills (TX7, TX3, TX9) stacked on filled vias, exactly as
drawn. Please keep them as separate drills; do not merge them into an L2-L5
drill.
