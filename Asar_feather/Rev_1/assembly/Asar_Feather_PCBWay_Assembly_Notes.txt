CPL AND ORIENTATION
Asar_Feather_PCBWay_CPL.csv: Designator, Mid X, Mid Y, Layer (Top/Bottom), Rotation.
It lists every fitted part with SMD pads (X1 included); X2 (shipped loose) and the DNS
test pads are not in it.
If your line needs one file per side, please split it by the Layer column.
- Units are millimetres ("mm" suffix). Origin: the board's lower-left
  corner; the outline runs from 0,0 to 50.80,22.86.
- Coordinates on both sides are as viewed from the top. Bottom-side X
  values are NOT mirrored.
- Mid X/Y is the centre of each part's pad extents, not the footprint
  origin. For connectors (X1, X3, X4, X5) align the part to its pads in CAM.
- Rotations are the values stored in the final Altium PCB, in degrees
  (0/90/180/270), not converted for bottom-side parts. Please confirm your
  bottom-side convention against the bottom drawing before programming.
- The top drawing is viewed from the top; the bottom drawing is mirrored,
  viewed from the bottom. Red dot = pin 1/A1; red bar = LED cathode (K);
  dashed red label = DNP. Most designators are hidden on the silkscreen.
  The silkscreen marks polarity with a dot beside pin 1 (A1 on U1 and U3)
  of U1, U2, U3, U4, Q1, BTN, RESET, X4 and X5 and beside each LED cathode;
  U7's dot is under its body (bottom side), between its pad rows at the pin-1
  end, so it is hidden once U7 is placed; U8 and U10 have a tick beside pin 1.
  The drawings and this file remain the orientation reference.
If the CPL and a drawing disagree, please send an engineering query.

SPECIAL COMPONENT INSTRUCTIONS
- Reflow order: bottom side FIRST, top side LAST, so U1, U3 and X1 (all on top)
  see one reflow. The bottom side then rides the second reflow upside down:
  its heaviest parts are X3 (microSD socket), U8 and the 2520 inductors; support
  or glue them if your process needs it.
- X1 (Amphenol ICC 10118193-0001LF, micro-USB B receptacle, top side): the
  mouth overhangs the left (USB) board edge by 0.70 mm and the
  flange by 1.30 mm; the maker's PCB-end line is on the board edge. Please
  apply Amphenol's published reflow profile (customer drawing 10118193). The
  five contacts and the four SMT shell tabs MP1-MP4 are reflowed. The two
  shell legs go into plated 0.50 x 1.20 mm slots MH1/MH2; the top stencil
  has no paste on them, so hand-solder them with an iron after reflow to
  full barrel fill (paste-in-hole is acceptable if you prefer). No selective
  mini-wave: bottom-side C149 (0805, GND and VU pads 0.40 mm apart) sits
  0.23 mm from MH1's land and would re-melt and bridge.
- X2 (Feather headers, 1x16 + 1x12, 2.54 mm): SHIPPED LOOSE. Buy PRPC016SAAN-RC x1,
  PRPC012SAAN-RC x1 (Sullins, straight male, gold) per the BOM, do NOT solder them,
  and pack one set with each finished board. Leave X2's 28 plated holes empty (no
  paste, no solder, no masking residue in the holes): we solder the headers ourselves.
- X5 (SM04B-SRSS-TB(LF)(SN), STEMMA QT, top side): the mouth faces the right board
  edge. Pin 1 (GND) is the south contact; check against the top drawing.
- X4 (JST S2B-PH-SM4-TB(LF)(SN), side entry, top side): it lies flat with the mouth
  facing the top board edge (the edge with the BAT / EN / USB header
  pins); the two fixing tabs sit at that edge. Pin 1 (the east contact) is
  battery positive, pin 2 is GND.
- X3 (Hirose DM3D-SF microSD, bottom side): per our placement record the card opening
  faces the right board edge; please check it against the bottom drawing.
- U2 (FT2232HQ-REEL, QFN-64), U4 (W25Q128JVPIQ, WSON-8) and U10
  (93LC46BT-I/MNY, TDFN-8): their exposed-pad lands have no net; the paste
  layer prints them 1:1. Please apply your windowpane reduction.
- U8 (LTC3556EUFD#PBF, Analog Devices, bottom side): QFN-28 4 x 5 mm, 0.5 mm
  pitch, exposed pad (pin 29) to GND. Pin 1 per the drawing's red dot and
  the silkscreen tick west of pad 1.
- U7 (Semtech SC189ASKTRT, SOT-23-5, bottom side): 1.0 V FPGA core buck. Pin 1 per the
  drawing's red dot and the silkscreen dot under the body, between the pad rows at the
  pin-1 end.
- Inductors L1, L2, L3, L8, L9 (bottom side) share one 2.5 x 2.0 mm body and
  are usually unmarked: L1, L2, L9 = DFE252012F-2R2M=P2 (2.2 uH), L3 =
  DFE252010P-1R5M=P2 (1.5 uH), L8 = DFE252012F-3R3M=P2 (3.3 uH). Load each
  position from its own reel and check the reels on the first article.
- 0603 LEDs: LD1, LD2: cathode (pad K) on the WEST side (X 31.20, 34.40 mm); LD3, LD4:
  cathode (pad K) on the EAST side (X 6.33 mm). The two pairs face opposite ways; the
  drawings mark each cathode with a red bar, the silkscreen with a dot beside pad K.
- LD0 (Everlight 19-337/R6GHBHC-A01/2T, 1.6 x 1.6 mm): cathodes are on the WEST side,
  the cathode-mark column (board footprint pads 2, 4, 6), anodes opposite (pads 1, 3,
  5). The drawing's red bar and the silkscreen dot mark that cathode column. The land
  is symmetric, so a part turned 180 degrees fits but stays dark.
  Its datasheet's pin numbers depend on the revision: Rev.4 (DSE-0002657)
  numbers the cathodes 1-3 and the anodes 4-6, Rev.6 numbers them like the
  footprint. Follow the cathode mark, not the pad numbers.
- BTN and RESET (PTS810SJM250SMTR LFS, top side): same part twice; pin 1 per the
  drawing's red dot and the silkscreen dot.
- JP3 and JP4 are intentional DNS JTAG test-pad positions (three 1.0 mm SMD
  pads each, bottom side): no part. Please remove their six apertures from
  the bottom stencil (tinned pads are acceptable if you cannot).
- Verify pin 1/A1 against the drawings on Q1, U1, U2, U3, U4, U7, U8, U10, X4 and X5.
- Stencil from the GTP/GBP layers, whose apertures are 1:1 with copper.
  Please apply your house reductions, in particular: U1 0.25 mm round
  apertures (0.08 mm or thinner stencil suggested, paste that releases
  them reliably); U3 0.40 mm lands; the exposed pads of U2, U4, U8 and U10.
