Generated by tools/bom_audit.py on 2026-11-20. Read on every run: the seven Altium sheets (components, parameters, pins), the Protel netlist Imported zulu_a7.PrjPcb/Project Outputs for zulu_a7_fab_b/zulu_a7_fab_b.NET (file dated 2026-11-20 14:20) and AMD's CPG236 pinout file. Computed from them on every run: section 1, the pin and net checks of 2.1 and 2.2, the capacitor and resistor derating of 5.1 and 5.2 (against worst-case rail voltages, the LTC3556 ones computed from its feedback dividers per docs/ltc3556.md), and which static rows print: a row of sections 2.3, 3, 4 or 5.3 prints only while its part, MPN or footprint is on the current sheets. Typed into the script, not read: the datasheet pin tables 2.2 checks against, the land-pattern table of 2.3, and the text of sections 3, 4, 5.3 and 6. That text is a 2026-09 audit snapshot unless the row, its part cell or a note carries a later date; rows rewritten for this board carry (2026-11-20). Distributor data is the Digi-Key snapshot of the evening of 2026-09-06. tools/ and docs/ paths named in rows and findings dated 2026-09 are in the original Altium project, Zulu_Altium_VS_Code/, not in this folder.

At 0.5 mm pitch the inter-land gap is 0.5 minus the land, and rings 1 and 2 must cross a populated row to reach the depopulated annulus whichever way they leave, so a 3 mil trace at 3.5 mil clearance (0.0762 + 2 x 0.09 = 0.2562 mm) has to fit through it. At a 0.225 mm land the gap is 0.275 mm and the margin is +0.0188 mm; at 0.275 mm the gap is 0.225 mm and the margin is -0.0312 mm. No layer count fixes that -- only via-in-pad HDI would, and PCBWay declined HDI in writing on 2026-08-27. The change was reverted the same day: the library carries 0.225 mm and tools/verify_pcblib.py asserts it. The trade is a 0.82:1 land against AMD's 1:1 board-level-reliability recommendation, and it had already been made deliberately in board/STACKUP.md on 2026-08-30.
