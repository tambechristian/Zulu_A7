Off the board. The four Creative Commons marks are licence art with no copper, carried through the import as real pinless components. They are not meant to be on the PCB, and they are not: all four sit below the outline on a shared baseline at y −9.502 mm, which is where the previous layout parked them. They stay in the design rather than being deleted, because the schematic still owns them and the next Import Changes would bring them straight back.

Still to re-cut. The 0201s under the ball field are on an even 6 × 4 lattice, assigned by rail rather than by designator: the eight VCC1V0 balls sit in a 0.5 × 3.0 mm cluster at the centre of the die, and the four core caps are 0.65 to 2.89 mm from it. That lattice will want re-cutting once the BGA fan-out is drawn.
